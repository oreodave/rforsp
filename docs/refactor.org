* Current state
** Implemented
- [[file:../src/lowering/lower.rs]] exports
  ~lower(forms, primitives, variables)~,
  returning ~(Option<Module>, Diagnostics)~.  ~Lowerer~ owns a module, lexical
  environment, work stack, and current ~BodyId~/~BlockId~.
- Empty input and integer, list, and quote forms lower successfully.  Each
  executable data form emits ~PushData~ with its source origin.  An outer quote
  pushes its operand's datum; quotes within datum content retain wrappers.
- Datum construction uses a separate explicit work stack.  Module-owned nodes
  reference children by ~DatumId~.  Tests cover order, empty containers, quoted
  atoms and vectors, and construction/destruction of deeply nested data.
- [[file:../src/lowering/module.rs]] creates entry body zero;
  [[file:../src/lowering/body.rs]] creates entry block zero.  Bodies own local
  counters and deduplicated capture layouts.  Blocks default to ~End~.
- [[file:../src/lowering/env.rs]] maintains one frame per body and nested scopes
  mapping ~SymId~ to body-local ~LocalId~.  ~bind(sym, local)~ registers a slot
  allocated by the caller.  ~resolve(sym, module)~ returns a local, capture, or
  primordial target, forwarding captures through every intervening body.
- Environment tests cover shadowing/restoration, equal local IDs in different
  bodies, nearest-frame lookup, transitive capture order and deduplication, and
  primordial fallback.  ~is_primordial~ checks shadowing without capturing.
- ~Work::FinishBody~ closes the environment frame, checks its body identity, and
  restores the saved parent body and block when present.  The entry sentinel
  closes the initial frame without a parent continuation.
** Incomplete integration
- ~Lowerer~ currently uses its environment only to initialise and close the
  entry frame.  Binding, load, call, and executable-vector lowering are still
  ~todo!~.  Nested-body entry and its saved continuation are not exercised yet.
- ~Work::Body~ splits off one form before lowering it.  Prefix recognition has
  not been integrated.
- The return boundary withholds the module when diagnostics contain errors,
  but lowering does not yet report name/recognition errors or exercise recovery.
  Unsupported executable forms currently panic rather than report diagnostics.
- [[file:../src/lib.rs]] disables the resolution module.  Its source and
  tests are retained under ~src/resolution/~ as migration reference, outside the
  active build.  The old binding table and resolution map are not lowering
  dependencies.
- [[file:../src/drivers.rs]] currently stops after parsing and HIR logging.  It
  invokes neither retained resolution nor new lowering.  [[file:compiler.org]]
  describes the old resolution-table boundary and needs reconciliation after
  integration.
* Responsibility split
~Lowerer~ traverses HIR, determines declaration visibility and scope boundaries,
allocates locals through ~Body::locals~, constructs bodies/blocks/instructions,
and reports source-backed diagnostics.  ~Environment~ maintains lexical state,
registers declarations, and resolves references, including module-mutating
capture propagation.  The lowerer emits the returned ~Target~ without managing
that propagation itself.

A binding's identity is its owning body plus local slot.  Keep the current
frame/scopes representation; the combined phase needs no separate ~BindingId~
or ~BindingTable~.  ~Module~ owns bodies throughout construction.  Captures grow
in those bodies during resolution, with no layout transfer at ~FinishBody~.

Preserve the retained recogniser's cases:
- Primordial ~<vector> rec~ recognises a recursive closure, consuming two forms.
- Integer, quote, or list before primordial ~rec~ recognises an invalid data
  operand.  Bind/load/call operands use the ordinary dynamic path.
- Primordial ~<then> <else> if~ consumes three forms when both operands are
  integers, quotes, lists, or vectors.  Bind/load/call operands remain dynamic.
- Lexically shadowed ~rec~ and ~if~ remain ordinary references.

After recognition selects a closure, allocate its ~Body~ to obtain ~BodyId~ and
emit ~MakeClosure~ immediately in the parent block, with the selected mode.
Save the parent body/block in ~FinishBody~, open the child's environment frame,
select its entry block, and schedule its forms ahead of the parent remainder.
The referenced capture layout may grow after emission.

Exercise empty and nested closures, parent continuation after a child, captured
loads/calls, and nested demand that adds captures to an otherwise unused middle
body.  ~FinishBody~ needs neither capture finalisation nor an extra ~End~ write
for the existing straight-line block representation.
